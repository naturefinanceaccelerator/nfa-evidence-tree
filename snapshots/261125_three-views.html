Reading notes
  • Colour shows detection frequency across samples. It never shows read counts.
  • A difference of one or two weeks is noise with a single sampler per site, so it gets no symbol.
  • Species symbols: + from 3 weeks, ++ from 5, +++ from 7. Group symbols use the group mean: + from 1 week, ++ from 2, +++ from 3. All thresholds are placeholders until DNAir provides replicate variance.
  • Plus means more detections, not better. Colour carries the judgement: for invasive species plus plant pathogens, less is the wanted direction.
  • Air mixes. A reference sampler close to the project catches partly the same DNA, so differences shrink.
  • Plants plus fungi show up in most weeks through pollen plus spores. Animals show up in fewer weeks, so their branches are paler.
  • Amphibians plus earthworms give a weak airborne signal. Bioacoustics plus the farmer's earthworm counts stay the lead methods there.
  • Flags are illustrative. Real flags come from the Swiss Red Lists plus the federal list of invasive alien species.
